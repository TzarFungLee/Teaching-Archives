📚 HKDSE Listening - Key Vocabulary

Word/Phrase Common Usage in Listening 中文意思 / Examples
時間表達 | Time Expressions
3 o'clock / quarter past / half past "Meet me at 3 o'clock sharp"
"The train leaves at quarter past two"
"Let's meet at half past six" 		三點正 / 兩點十五分 / 六點半
Listen for exact times in appointments and schedules
in the morning / afternoon / evening "The meeting is in the afternoon"
"Classes start in the morning"
"We'll arrive in the evening" 		早上 / 下午 / 晚上
Common in daily schedule discussions
by / before / after / around "Submit by Friday"
"Come before 9am"
"Call me after 5pm"
"I'll be there around noon" 		在...之前 / 在...之後 / 大約
Time prepositions - listen carefully!
postpone / delay / reschedule "We need to postpone the event"
"The flight was delayed by 2 hours"
"Can we reschedule for next week?" 		延期；推遲；延誤；改期
Schedule changes are common
香港地點與港鐵 | HK Places & MTR
Central / Admiralty / Tsim Sha Tsui "Let's meet at Central Station"
"Transfer at Admiralty"
"Get off at Tsim Sha Tsui" 		中環 / 金鐘 / 尖沙咀
Major MTR interchange stations
Mong Kok / Causeway Bay / Wan Chai "The shop is near Mong Kok"
"Meet me at Causeway Bay"
"Exit at Wan Chai station" 		旺角 / 銅鑼灣 / 灣仔
Popular shopping/business districts
Exit A / Exit B / Platform 1 "Use Exit A for the mall"
"Meet at Exit B"
"Board on Platform 1" 		A出口 / B出口 / 1號月台
MTR directions - very common!
Island Line / Tsuen Wan Line "Take the Island Line to Chai Wan"
"Change to Tsuen Wan Line"
"The Kwun Tong Line is faster" 		港島線 / 荃灣線 / 觀塘線
MTR line names
數字與價錢 | Numbers & Prices
$50 / $150 / $500 "It costs fifty dollars"
"The ticket is one hundred and fifty"
"Rent is five hundred per month" 		五十元 / 一百五十元 / 五百元
Listen for prices carefully
discount / 20% off / half price "Get a 20% discount"
"It's 20% off today"
"Everything is half price" 		折扣 / 八折 / 半價
Shopping contexts
dozen / a couple of / several "Buy a dozen eggs" (12)
"A couple of days" (2-3)
"Several people came" (3-7) 		一打（12個）/ 幾個 / 數個
Quantity expressions
學校與活動 | School & Activities
assignment / deadline / submit "Hand in the assignment by Friday"
"The deadline is next Monday"
"Please submit your work online" 		作業；功課 / 截止日期 / 提交
Academic contexts
sign up / register / enroll "Sign up for the club"
"Register before Friday"
"Enroll in the course" 		報名；登記；註冊
Activities and courses
cancel / withdraw / drop "We have to cancel the event"
"I need to withdraw from the class"
"Can I drop this course?" 		取消 / 退出 / 放棄
Changing plans
方向與位置 | Directions & Locations
turn left / right / go straight "Turn left at the traffic lights"
"Turn right after the bank"
"Go straight for 2 blocks" 		左轉 / 右轉 / 直走
Basic directions - very common
opposite / next to / between "It's opposite the post office"
"The cafe is next to the bank"
"It's between the library and park" 		對面 / 旁邊 / 之間
Location prepositions
on the corner / at the end of "The shop is on the corner"
"Walk to the end of the street"
"It's at the end of the corridor" 		在轉角處 / 在...盡頭
Specific locations
電話與訊息 | Telephone & Messages
leave a message / call back / get back to "Can I leave a message?"
"I'll call you back later"
"I'll get back to you tomorrow" 		留言 / 回電 / 回覆你
Phone conversations
hold on / hang up / put through "Hold on, please" (wait)
"Don't hang up"
"I'll put you through to the manager" 		請稍等 / 掛斷 / 轉接
Phone etiquette
天氣與狀況 | Weather & Conditions
sunny / cloudy / rainy / typhoon "It'll be sunny tomorrow"
"Expect cloudy skies"
"It's rainy today"
"There's a typhoon warning" 		晴天 / 多雲 / 下雨 / 颱風
HK weather - typhoons common!
degrees / temperature / humid "It's 25 degrees today"
"The temperature will drop"
"It's very humid in summer" 		度 / 溫度 / 潮濕
Weather descriptions
常見動詞 | Common Verbs
arrange / organize / prepare "I'll arrange a meeting"
"Can you organize the event?"
"We need to prepare for the exam" 		安排 / 組織 / 準備
Planning activities
confirm / check / make sure "Please confirm your booking"
"Let me check the schedule"
"Make sure you bring your ID" 		確認 / 檢查 / 確保
Verification
remind / inform / notify "Can you remind me tomorrow?"
"I'll inform you later"
"We'll notify you by email" 		提醒 / 通知 / 告知
Communication
