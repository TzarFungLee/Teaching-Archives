Key Strategies (主要策略)

📝 Strategy 1: Note-Taking Skills (筆記技巧)

✓ How to take notes while listening: (聆聽時如何做筆記)
  • Use abbreviations (使用縮寫): "w/" = with, "b/c" = because, "ur" = your/you are, "2" = to/too
  • Use symbols (使用符號): "↑" = increase, "↓" = decrease, "→" = next/then, "?" = question/confused
  • Write only keywords (只寫關鍵詞): NOT full sentences (不要寫完整句子). Example: "John - 25 years - teacher - London"
  • Write phonetically if necessary (必要時用拼音): If you don't know the spelling, write how it sounds (如果不知道拼法，就寫出聲音)

📝 Strategy 2: Read the Questions FIRST (先讀題目)

IMPORTANT: Read all questions and answer choices BEFORE you listen! (重要：聆聽前先閱讀所有題目和選項！)

You will have time to read the questions. Use this time wisely: (你會有時間閱讀題目。明智地使用這段時間)

  • Underline key words in the questions (在題目中劃出關鍵詞)
  • Know what information you need to listen for (知道你需要聆聽什麼資訊)
  • Look for numbers, names, dates, places - these are important (留意數字、名字、日期、地點 - 這些很重要)
  • Read answer choices to understand what to expect (閱讀答案選項以了解會聽到什麼)

📝 Strategy 3: Prediction Technique (預測技巧)

Predict what you will hear: (預測你會聽到什麼)

After reading the questions, try to guess what the speaker will say. This helps your brain prepare to listen for the right information. (閱讀題目後，嘗試猜測說話者會說什麼。這有助於你的大腦準備好聆聽正確的資訊)

  • If the question asks "Where does she work?", think about possible places: hospital, school, office, shop... (如果問題問「她在哪裡工作？」，想想可能的地方：醫院、學校、辦公室、商店...)
  • If the question asks "How much does it cost?", think about numbers you might hear (如果問題問「多少錢？」，想想你可能會聽到的數字)
  • If the question asks about time, think about times: morning, afternoon, 3 o'clock, next week... (如果問題問時間，想想時間：早上、下午、三點、下星期...)

📝 Strategy 4: Active Listening - Focus on Details (主動聆聽 - 專注細節)

Listen for specific information: (聆聽特定資訊)
  • Numbers (數字) (prices, times, dates, ages): Listen carefully. These often change. (價錢、時間、日期、年齡：仔細聆聽。這些經常會變)
  • Names (名字) (people, places): These may be difficult to spell. Just listen for the sound. (人名、地名：可能很難拼寫。只需聆聽聲音)
  • Opinion words (意見詞): "I think...", "I like...", "I don't like..." - These tell you what the speaker believes. (這些告訴你說話者的想法)
  • Reason words (原因詞): "because", "so", "due to" - These explain WHY something happened. (這些解釋為什麼發生某事)
  • Contrast words (對比詞): "but", "however", "although" - These show the opposite idea. (這些顯示相反的意思)

📝 Strategy 5: What to Do When You Don't Understand (當你不明白時怎麼辦)

❌ DO NOT PANIC if you don't understand: (如果你不明白，不要恐慌)
  • Move on (繼續前進): The speaker may say the information again later (說話者可能會稍後再說一次)
  • Use context (使用上下文): Think about the general topic. Guess based on context. (想想整體話題。根據上下文猜測)
  • Answer other questions (回答其他問題): Answer the questions you CAN understand. Come back to difficult ones later. (回答你能明白的問題。稍後再回來處理困難的)
  • Guess if necessary (必要時猜測): An answer that might be wrong is better than no answer (可能錯的答案總比沒有答案好)
