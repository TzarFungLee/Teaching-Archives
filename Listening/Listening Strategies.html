🎧 HKDSE Listening - Test Strategies

Master techniques to improve your score on Paper 1

Before You Start - Preparation Tips

✓ Get Ready for the Exam:
  • Sleep well: Get 8 hours of sleep the night before. Your ears need to be fresh to understand English.
  • Eat breakfast: Eat a good breakfast. Low energy = poor listening.
  • Check your ears: Clean your ears. Make sure you can hear clearly.
  • Practice listening: Listen to English every day. Watch English movies, podcasts, and news.
  • Relax: Do not be nervous. Nervousness makes it hard to listen.

During the Exam - Key Strategies

📝 Strategy 1: Read the Questions FIRST

IMPORTANT: Read all questions and answer choices BEFORE you listen!

You will have time to read the questions. Use this time wisely:

  • Underline key words in the questions
  • Know what information you need to listen for
  • Look for numbers, names, dates, places - these are important
  • Read answer choices to understand what to expect

📝 Strategy 2: Prediction Technique

Predict what you will hear:

After reading the questions, try to guess what the speaker will say. This helps your brain prepare to listen for the right information.

  • If the question asks "Where does she work?", think about possible places: hospital, school, office, shop...
  • If the question asks "How much does it cost?", think about numbers you might hear
  • If the question asks about time, think about times: morning, afternoon, 3 o'clock, next week...

📝 Strategy 3: Active Listening - Focus on Details

Listen for specific information:
  • Numbers (prices, times, dates, ages): Listen carefully. These often change.
  • Names (people, places): These may be difficult to spell. Just listen for the sound.
  • Opinion words: "I think...", "I like...", "I don't like..." - These tell you what the speaker believes.
  • Reason words: "because", "so", "due to" - These explain WHY something happened.
  • Contrast words: "but", "however", "although" - These show the opposite idea.

📝 Strategy 4: Note-Taking Skills

✓ How to take notes while listening:
  • Use abbreviations: "w/" = with, "b/c" = because, "ur" = your/you are, "2" = to/too
  • Use symbols: "↑" = increase, "↓" = decrease, "→" = next/then, "?" = question/confused
  • Write only keywords: NOT full sentences. Example: "John - 25 years - teacher - London"
  • Write phonetically if necessary: If you don't know the spelling, write how it sounds
  • Listen first, write later: Do NOT write during critical information. Listen FIRST, then write what you remember

📝 Strategy 5: Understanding Different Accents

✓ Handle accent differences:
  • British English, American English, Australian English - all sound different
  • Listen to accents regularly to get used to them
  • Focus on the MEANING, not perfect pronunciation
  • If you miss one word, do NOT worry. Keep listening for the next information.

📝 Strategy 6: What to Do When You Don't Understand

❌ DO NOT PANIC if you don't understand:
  • Move on: The speaker may say the information again later
  • Use context: Think about the general topic. Guess based on context.
  • Answer other questions: Answer the questions you CAN understand. Come back to difficult ones later.
  • Guess if necessary: An answer that might be wrong is better than no answer

📝 Strategy 7: Time Management

⏱️ Use your time wisely:
  • Before listening: Read and predict (2-3 minutes)
  • During listening: Focus completely (20-25 minutes)
  • After listening: Check your answers and guess for blanks (2-3 minutes)
  • Do NOT worry about: Perfect spelling or writing. Speed is more important.

After the Exam

✓ Learn from your mistakes:
  • Review the test when you finish
  • Write down words you could not understand
  • Listen to the same audio again at home
  • Practice with more past papers
