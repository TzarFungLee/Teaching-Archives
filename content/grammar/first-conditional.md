        <h3>💡 Key Concepts</h3>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>When to use:</strong> Talk about real and possible situations in the future</li>
            <li><strong>Structure:</strong> <mark class="cyan">If + present simple</mark>, <mark class="red">will + base verb</mark></li>
            <ul>
                <li><strong>If clause (condition):</strong> Uses <mark class="green">present simple</mark> to describe a possible situation</li>
                <li><strong>Main clause (result):</strong> Uses <mark class="red">will + base verb</mark> to describe what will happen</li>
                <li><strong>Example:</strong> <mark class="cyan">If it <mark class="green">rains</mark></mark>, <mark class="yellow">we <mark class="red">will stay</mark> home</mark>.</li>
            </ul>
            <li><strong>Reminders:</strong> 
                <ul>
                    <li>Never use "will" in the if-clause</li>
                    <li>Use a comma when the if-clause comes first</li>
                    <li>Can use other modals (can, may, might, should, must) instead of "will"</li>
                </ul>
            </li>
        </ul>

            </tr>

        <h3>📖 Examples by Category</h3>

        <h4>Predictions</h4>
        <ul>
            <li><mark class="cyan">If it <mark class="green">snows</mark> tomorrow</mark>, <mark class="yellow">the schools <mark class="red">will close</mark></mark>.</li>
            <li><mark class="cyan">If you <mark class="green">eat</mark> too much</mark>, <mark class="yellow">you <mark class="red">will feel</mark> sick</mark>.</li>
            <li><mark class="cyan">If he <mark class="green">doesn't hurry</mark></mark>, <mark class="yellow">he <mark class="red">will miss</mark> the bus</mark>.</li>
        </ul>

        <h4>Warnings</h4>
        <ul>
            <li><mark class="cyan">If you <mark class="green">touch</mark> that hot pan</mark>, <mark class="yellow">you <mark class="red">will burn</mark> yourself</mark>.</li>
            <li><mark class="cyan">If you <mark class="green">don't wear</mark> a coat</mark>, <mark class="yellow">you <mark class="red">will get</mark> cold</mark>.</li>
            <li><mark class="cyan">If we <mark class="green">don't leave</mark> now</mark>, <mark class="yellow">we <mark class="red">will be</mark> late</mark>.</li>
        </ul>

        <h4>Promises and Offers</h4>
        <ul>
            <li><mark class="cyan">If you <mark class="green">help</mark> me</mark>, <mark class="yellow">I <mark class="red">will buy</mark> you lunch</mark>.</li>
            <li><mark class="cyan">If you <mark class="green">come</mark> to the party</mark>, <mark class="yellow">I <mark class="red">will introduce</mark> you to my friends</mark>.</li>
            <li><mark class="cyan">If you <mark class="green">finish</mark> early</mark>, <mark class="yellow">we <mark class="red">will go</mark> to the cinema</mark>.</li>
        </ul>

        <h3>⚠️ Common Mistakes</h3>
        <table>
            <tr>
                <th>❌ Incorrect</th>
                <th>✅ Correct</th>
            </tr>
            <tr>
                <td>If it <mark class="red">will rain</mark>, we will stay home.</td>
                <td><mark class="cyan">If it <mark class="green">rains</mark></mark>, <mark class="yellow">we <mark class="red">will stay</mark> home</mark>.</td>
            </tr>
            <tr>
                <td>If you study, you <span style="text-decoration: line-through;">pass</span> the exam.</td>
                <td><mark class="cyan">If you <mark class="green">study</mark></mark>, <mark class="yellow">you <mark class="red">will pass</mark> the exam</mark>.</td>
            </tr>
            <tr>
                <td>If she <mark class="red">will come</mark>, I will go.</td>
                <td><mark class="cyan">If she <mark class="green">comes</mark></mark>, <mark class="yellow">I <mark class="red">will go</mark></mark>.</td>
            </tr>
            <tr>
                <td>We will go to park, if it is sunny. (wrong comma)</td>
                <td><mark class="yellow">We <mark class="red">will go</mark> to the park</mark> <mark class="cyan">if it <mark class="green">is</mark> sunny</mark>. (no comma)</td>
            </tr>
