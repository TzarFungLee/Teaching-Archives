⚖️ Argumentative Essay

  • Clear thesis in introduction.
  • 2–3 body paragraphs with topic sentences and examples.
  • Include at least one counter-argument + refutation.

✉️ Letter to the Editor

  • Start with "Dear Editor," and a clear purpose sentence.
  • Formal, polite but critical tone.
  • Close with "Yours faithfully," + full name.

📋 Proposal

  • Sections: Purpose, Background, Suggestions, Expected Benefits.
  • Use bullet points for suggestions.
  • Emphasize feasibility and benefits to school / students.

📊 Report

  • Headings: Introduction, Findings, Analysis, Recommendations.
  • Formal, objective tone; often past tense.
  • Use data or survey results if given.

📰 Magazine / School Article

  • Catchy title and engaging hook in first paragraph.
  • Semi-formal tone with clear paragraphs.
  • End with a message, suggestion, or reflection.

🎤 Speech

  • Opening: greeting + purpose ("Good morning teachers and fellow students…").
  • Use rhetorical questions and direct address ("you / all of you").
  • Strong call to action in conclusion.

📖 Short Story

  • Clear setting, characters, and problem.
  • Use descriptive details (show, don't tell).
  • Finish with a resolution or twist ending.

💻 Blog Post

  • Chatty, personal tone; can use "I" and "you".
  • Share opinions, experiences, and questions to readers.
  • End with a quick reflection or invitation to comment.
