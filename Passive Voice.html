Passive Voice

Passive Voice Guide

💡 Key Concepts

  • Active voice: Subject performs the action (The teacher explains the rule).
  • Passive voice: Subject receives the action (The rule is explained by the teacher).
  • When to use: Emphasize the action, unknown agent, or formal writing
  • Structure: Subject + BE verb + past participle + (by + agent)

📖 Examples

Active Voice Passive Voice
Present Tense
She writes the report. The report is written by her.
They complete the project. The project is completed by them.
The news shocks everyone. Everyone is shocked by the news.
Past Tense
She wrote the report. The report was written by her.
They completed the project. The project was completed by them.
The news shocked everyone. Everyone was shocked by the news.
Future Tense
She will write the report. The report will be written by her.
They will complete the project. The project will be completed by them.
The news will shock everyone. Everyone will be shocked by the news.
