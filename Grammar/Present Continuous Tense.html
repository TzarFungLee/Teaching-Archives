💡 Key Concepts

  • When to use: Actions happening now, temporary situations, future arrangements
  • Structure: Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing + (object/complement)
  • Examples:
    • I am studying English right now.
    • She is working on a project.
    • They are playing soccer at the moment.
  • Spelling rules: drop final 'e' (make→making), double consonant (run→running), change 'ie' to 'y' (lie→lying)
  • Time markers: now, right now, at the moment, currently, today, this week

📦 Concept Compressed

The Present Continuous (also called Present Progressive) describes actions happening at the moment of speaking or ongoing temporary situations. It uses am/is/are + verb-ing.

When to Use

  • Actions happening now: She is reading a book right now.
  • Temporary situations: I'm living with my parents this month.
  • Future arrangements: We're meeting at 7 PM tomorrow.
  • Changing situations: The weather is getting colder.

Formation

Subject Be Form Verb + ing Example
I am working I am working.
He/She/It is working She is working.
You/We/They are working They are working.

Spelling Rules for -ing

  • Most verbs: Add -ing (play → playing)
  • Verbs ending in -e: Drop -e, add -ing (make → making)
  • Short verbs (CVC pattern): Double final consonant (run → running, sit → sitting)
  • Verbs ending in -ie: Change to -y (lie → lying, die → dying)

Common Time Markers

Now, right now, at the moment, currently, today, this week, these days

Key Rules

  • Stative verbs: Don't use continuous form with verbs of state (know, believe, love, want, need)
  • Negatives: am/is/are + not + verb-ing
  • Questions: Am/Is/Are + subject + verb-ing?
