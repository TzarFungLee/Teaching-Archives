📦 Concept Compressed

Unless means "if not" or "except if." It introduces a negative condition—the exception that would prevent the main clause from happening.

Meaning and Usage

Unless = If... not

  • Unless you hurry, you will be late. = If you do not hurry, you will be late.
  • We will go to the beach unless it rains. = We will go to the beach if it does not rain.

Structure

Unless + subject + present simple, subject + will/modal + base verb

Conversion Table

If Not Unless
If you do not leave now... Unless you leave now...
If it does not rain... Unless it rains...

Key Rules

  • Use present simple after "unless": Unless it rains...
  • Don't use "not" with "unless": Unless you study (NOT Unless you do not study)
