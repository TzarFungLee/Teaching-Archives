Defining Relative Clauses (Essential Info)
The teacher / teaches English / is kind The teacher who teaches English is kind. 🔊
The book / I borrowed / is on the table The book __________ I borrowed is on the table. The book that I borrowed is on the table. 🔊
Non-Defining Relative Clauses (Extra Info)
My brother / lives in Tokyo / is a doctor My brother, who lives in Tokyo, is a doctor. 🔊

📦 Concept Compressed

Defining relative clauses identify which person or thing we are talking about. Non-defining clauses add extra information about someone or something we already know.

Key Differences

Aspect Defining Non-Defining
Commas No Yes ,
Purpose Essential ID Extra Detail
Pronouns Who, Which, That Who, Which (No That)

Important Rules

  • No commas in defining clauses: The man who lives there...
  • Commas in non-defining clauses: My uncle, who lives there, ...
  • That: Never use that in a non-defining clause.
