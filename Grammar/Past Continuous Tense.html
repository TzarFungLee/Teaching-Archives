📦 Concept Compressed

The Past Continuous (also called Past Progressive) describes actions that were in progress at a specific moment in the past. It uses was/were + verb-ing.

Formation

  • Structure (Affirmative): Subject + was/were + Verb-ing + Object
  • Structure (Negative): Subject + was/were not + Verb-ing + Object
  • Structure (Question): Was/Were + Subject + Verb-ing + Object?

When to Use

  • Action in progress at a specific past time: I was sleeping at 10 PM last night.
  • Interrupted action: She was cooking when the phone rang. (Past Continuous + when + Simple Past)
  • Two parallel actions: While I was studying, my brother was playing games.

Common Patterns

Pattern Example
Past Continuous + when + Simple Past I was walking when it started raining.
Past Continuous + while + Past Continuous She was reading while he was cooking.
