💡 Key Concepts

  • When to use: Actions in progress at a specific time in the past, interrupted actions, parallel actions
  • Structure: Subject + was/were + verb-ing + (object/complement)
  • Examples:
    • I was studying at 8pm last night.
    • She was reading a book when I called.
    • They were playing soccer while we were watching.
  • Common with: when (for interruption) and while (for parallel actions)
  • Time markers: at (time) yesterday, while, when, as, all day/night/morning

📦 Concept Compressed

The Past Continuous (also called Past Progressive) describes actions that were in progress at a specific moment in the past. It uses was/were + verb-ing and is often used with the Simple Past to show interrupted actions.

When to Use

  • Action in progress at a specific past time: I was sleeping at 10 PM last night.
  • Interrupted action: She was cooking when the phone rang. (Past Continuous + when + Simple Past)
  • Two parallel actions: While I was studying, my brother was playing games. (while + Past Continuous)
  • Background description: It was raining. The wind was blowing.

Formation

Subject Was/Were Verb + ing Example
I/He/She/It was studying I was studying.
You/We/They were studying They were studying.

Common Patterns

Pattern Use Example
Past Continuous + when + Simple Past Interrupted action I was walking when it started raining.
While + Past Continuous, Simple Past Background + main event While I was driving, I saw an accident.
Past Continuous + while + Past Continuous Two parallel actions She was reading while he was cooking.

Key Rules

  • Use with time expressions: at 5 PM yesterday, all morning, during the meeting
  • Negatives: was/were + not + verb-ing
  • Questions: Was/Were + subject + verb-ing?
