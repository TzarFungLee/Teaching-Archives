Past Continuous Tense

💡 Key Concepts

  • When to use: Actions in progress at a specific time in the past, interrupted actions, parallel actions
  • Structure: Subject + was/were + verb-ing + object/complement
  • Examples:
    • I was studying at 8pm last night.
    • She was reading a book when I called.
    • They were playing soccer while we were watching.
  • Common with: when (for interruption) and while (for parallel actions)
  • Time markers: at (time) yesterday, while, when, as, all day/night/morning
