Use for Actions in Progress in the past.

✅ Positive
was / were + Verb-ing
"I was sleeping at 10pm."
⚡ Interrupted
was -ing ... when ... Simple Past
"I was walking when it rained."

📦 Concept Compressed

The Past Continuous describes actions that were in progress at a specific moment in the past. It uses was/were + verb-ing.

Formation

  • Structure: Subject + was/were + Verb-ing + Object

When to Use

  • Action in progress: I was sleeping at 10 PM.
  • Interrupted action: She was cooking when the phone rang.
