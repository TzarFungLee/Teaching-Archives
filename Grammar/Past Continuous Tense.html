🕒 Past Continuous Roadmap

Use for Actions in Progress in the past.

✅ Positive
was / were + Verb-ing
"I was sleeping at 10pm."
⚡ Interrupted
was -ing ... when ... Simple Past
"I was walking when it rained."

Your Turn - Fill in the Blanks
cook / 6pm She __________ (was / cook) She was cooking dinner at 6pm yesterday. dinner at 6pm yesterday. 🔊
Interrupted Actions (with 'when')
study / called She __________ (was / study) She was studying when her friend called. when her friend called. 🔊
watch / went out We __________ (were / watch) We were watching a film when the lights went out. a film when the lights went out. 🔊
Two Actions Together (with 'while')
read / listen While I __________ (was / read), my sister __________ (was / listen) to music. While I was reading, my sister was listening to music. 🔊
Negatives & Questions
not / pay attention He __________ (was / not / pay) attention during the lesson. He was not paying attention during the lesson. 🔊
you / do What __________ you (do) at 10 pm last night? What were you doing at 10 pm last night? 🔊

📦 Concept Compressed

The Past Continuous describes actions that were in progress at a specific moment in the past. It uses was/were + verb-ing.

Formation

  • Structure: Subject + was/were + Verb-ing + Object

When to Use

  • Action in progress at a past moment: I was sleeping at 10 PM.
  • Interrupted action (long action + when + short action): She was cooking when the phone rang.
  • Two actions at the same time (while): While I was reading, my sister was listening to music.

Time Markers

at 8 pm yesterday, at that moment, when (+ simple past), while, all morning/afternoon/night, during the lesson
