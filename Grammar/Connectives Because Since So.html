Connectives: Because, Since, So

💡 Key Concepts

  • Because/Since: Show the reason for something (comes BEFORE the result)
  • So: Show the result of something (comes AFTER the reason)
  • Structure:
    • Reason + because/since + explanation
    • Explanation + so + result

📖 Practice

Using "because/since" Using "so"
I stayed home because I was sick. I was sick, so I stayed home.
She is happy ____________ she passed the exam. She passed the exam, ____________ she is happy.
We left early ____________ it started raining. It started raining, ____________ we left early.
He couldn't sleep ____________ he drank coffee. He drank coffee, ____________ he couldn't sleep.
They are studying hard ____________ they want good grades. They want good grades, ____________ they are studying hard.
