💡 Key Concepts

  • When to use: Completed actions in the past, past habits, historical events
  • Structure: Subject + verb (past form / -ed or irregular) + (object/complement)
  • Examples:
    • I visited London last year.
    • She wrote a letter yesterday.
    • They lived in Japan in 2010.
  • Time markers: yesterday, last (week/month/year), ago, in (year), when I was young

📦 Concept Compressed

The Simple Past Tense describes completed actions or states in the past. It uses the past form of verbs—either regular (-ed endings) or irregular forms.

When to Use

  • Completed actions: I visited Paris last summer.
  • Past habits: She studied every night when she was in college.
  • Historical events: World War II ended in 1945.
  • Sequential past actions: He woke up, ate breakfast, and left.

Formation

Verb Type Formation Example
Regular Base + ed walk → walked
Irregular Unique past form go → went, see → saw

Common Time Markers

Yesterday, last night/week/month/year, ago, in [past year], when I was..., during...

Key Rules

  • Regular verbs: Add -ed (jumped), -d (lived), double consonant + -ed (stopped), or -ied (studied)
  • Irregular verbs: Memorize unique forms (buy→bought, think→thought)
  • Negatives: Use did not (didn't) + base verb
  • Questions: Did + subject + base verb?
