Situation Modal Sentence
You _____ wear a uniform at school. (Rule) You __________ (rule) wear a uniform. You have to wear a uniform at school. 🔊
I _____ finish this homework. (Personal) I __________ (personal) finish this. I must finish this homework. 🔊
You _____ not smoke here. (Forbidden) You __________ (forbidden) smoke here. You must not smoke here. 🔊
They _____ not bring a gift. (Optional) They __________ (optional) bring a gift. They don't have to bring a gift. 🔊

📦 Concept Compressed

Must, have to, and need to all express obligation or necessity, but differ in formality, source of obligation, and meaning in negative forms.

Positive Forms: Obligation/Necessity

Modal Meaning Example
must Personal feeling / Rules I must finish this today.
have to External rule (School/Job) Students have to wear uniforms.
need to Neutral necessity I need to buy groceries.

Negative Forms: VERY DIFFERENT!

Negative Form Meaning Example
must not PROHIBITION (Forbidden) You mustn't smoke here.
don't have to OPTIONAL (Not necessary) You don't have to come.

Critical Rule

  • Must not: You are forbidden to do it.
  • Don't have to: You are not required to do it (it's your choice).
