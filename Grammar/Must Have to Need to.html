Must, Have to, Need to

💡 Key Concepts

  • All three show OBLIGATION or NECESSITY - something is required or important to do
  • Differences:
    • Must - Strong personal feeling, rules, or laws (more formal)
    • Have to - External obligation (someone else's rule)
    • Need to - Necessity (more neutral, less strong)
  • Negative forms:
    • Must not - Prohibition (NOT allowed)
    • Don't have to - NOT necessary (you can choose)
    • Don't need to - NOT necessary (you can choose)
