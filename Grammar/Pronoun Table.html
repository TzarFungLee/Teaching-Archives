📦 Concept Compressed

Pronouns replace nouns to avoid repetition. English pronouns change form based on their grammatical function.

Key Rules

  • Subject pronouns: Come before the verb.
  • Object pronouns: Come after the verb or preposition.
  • Possessive adjectives: Must be followed by a noun (my book).
  • Possessive pronouns: Stand alone (This is mine).
  • Reflexive pronouns: Use when subject and object are the same.
