📦 Concept Compressed

English tenses express when an action happens (time) and its aspect (duration, completion, etc.).

The Five Tenses in This Course

Tense Structure Use Time markers
Simple Present Verb (+s/es) Habits / Facts every day, always, usually
Present Continuous am/is/are + verb-ing Actions happening now now, right now, Look!
Simple Past Past form (-ed / irregular) Finished actions yesterday, last week, ago
Past Continuous was/were + verb-ing Action in progress in the past at 5 pm yesterday, while, when
Simple Future will + base verb Future actions / predictions tomorrow, next week, soon

Key Rules

  • Simple: Used for general facts and routines.
  • Continuous: Used for actions in progress at a moment in time.
  • Pick the tense from the time marker: every day → simple present · now → present continuous · yesterday → simple past · at 5 pm yesterday / when… → past continuous · tomorrow → simple future.
