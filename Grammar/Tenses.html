📦 Concept Compressed

English tenses express when an action happens (time) and its aspect (duration, completion, etc.).

The Main Tenses

Tense Structure Use
Simple Present Base Verb Habits/Facts
Present Continuous am/is/are + verb-ing Actions now
Simple Past Past form (-ed) Completed actions
Simple Future will + base verb Future actions

Key Rules

  • Simple: Used for general facts and routines.
  • Continuous: Used for actions in progress.
  • Perfect: Used for actions connected to another time.
