💡 Key Concepts

  • Purpose: Show direction or movement from one place to another
  • Common prepositions:
    • to - moving towards a destination
    • into - moving inside something
    • out of - moving from inside to outside
    • through - moving from one side to another
    • across - moving from one side to the opposite side
    • along - moving in a line beside something
    • up / down - moving higher/lower
    • over / under - moving above/below

📦 Concept Compressed

Prepositions of movement describe the direction or path of motion from one place to another. Each preposition indicates a specific type of movement relationship.

Common Prepositions of Movement

Preposition Meaning Example
to Towards a destination I'm going to school.
into From outside to inside She walked into the room.
out of From inside to outside He came out of the building.
through From one end to the other, passing inside We drove through the tunnel.
across From one side to the opposite side They walked across the bridge.
along Moving beside/following a line We walked along the river.
up / down Moving to a higher/lower position She ran up the stairs.
He walked down the hill.
over Moving above and across The cat jumped over the fence.
under Moving beneath The dog crawled under the table.
around Moving in a circle or to avoid We walked around the lake.
past Moving beyond a point She drove past the station.
towards Moving in the direction of (but not necessarily reaching) He's walking towards the park.

Key Distinctions

  • To vs. Into: "To" indicates destination; "into" emphasizes entering. (Go to the house vs. Walk into the house)
  • Through vs. Across: "Through" = inside and emerging; "across" = from one side to other on a surface. (Through a tunnel vs. Across a field)
  • Along vs. Across: "Along" = following beside; "across" = crossing from side to side. (Along the street vs. Across the street)
  • Over vs. Above: "Over" implies movement; "above" is static position. (Jump over the box vs. The lamp is above the table)

Usage with Verbs

Common verbs of movement: go, walk, run, drive, fly, swim, jump, climb, crawl

  • He climbed up the mountain.
  • They swam across the river.
  • She ran through the park.
