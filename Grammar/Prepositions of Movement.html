📦 Concept Compressed

Prepositions of movement describe the direction or path of motion from one place to another.

Common Prepositions

Preposition Meaning Example
to Towards a destination Go to school.
into Outside to inside Walk into the room.
through One side to other (inside) Drive through the tunnel.
across One side to other (surface) Walk across the bridge.
out of Inside to outside Run out of the building.
over Above and to the other side Jump over the fence.
up / down To a higher / lower place Climb up the ladder. Roll down the hill.
along Following a line or path Walk along the promenade.
around In a circle / on all sides Run around the playground.
past Beside and beyond something Walk past the 7-Eleven.

Key Distinctions

  • Through vs. Across: Through = inside something (a tunnel, a crowd); Across = on a surface (a road, a bridge).
  • Over vs. Above: Over implies movement; Above is a static position.
  • Into vs. In: Into = movement (walk into the room); In = position (sit in the room).
  • Past vs. Through: Past = you go beside it; Through = you go inside it.
