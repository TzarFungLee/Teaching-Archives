📦 Concept Compressed

These three connectives all express result or consequence, but differ in formality level and punctuation.

Formality Levels

  • so: Informal (Casual conversation)
  • therefore: Formal (Essays, Reports)
  • as a result: Formal (Academic writing)

Punctuation Patterns

  • Reason, so result.
  • Reason. Therefore, result.
  • Reason. As a result, result.

Key Differences

  • So: Connects two clauses with a comma; cannot start a sentence.
  • Therefore / As a result: Start a new sentence; require period before and comma after.
