Connectives: So, Therefore, As a result

💡 Key Concepts

  • Purpose: All three words show RESULT or CONSEQUENCE
  • Formality:
    • So - Informal, casual (speaking and informal writing)
    • Therefore - Formal (essays, reports, academic writing)
    • As a result - Formal (essays, reports, academic writing)
  • Punctuation:
    • So: Reason, so result.
    • Therefore: Reason. Therefore, result.
    • As a result: Reason. As a result, result.

📖 Practice

Informal (So) Formal (Therefore / As a result)
It was raining, so we stayed inside. It was raining. Therefore, we stayed inside.
She studied hard, _______ she passed the exam. She studied hard. ______________, she passed the exam.
He missed the bus, _______ he was late. He missed the bus. ______________, he was late.
The shop was closed, _______ we went home. The shop was closed. ______________, we went home.
They didn't have tickets, _______ they couldn't enter. They didn't have tickets. ______________, they couldn't enter.

📦 Concept Compressed

These three connectives all express result or consequence, but differ in formality level and punctuation. Choose based on your writing context.

Formality Levels

Connector Formality Use In Example
so Informal Conversation, emails, casual writing I was tired, so I went home.
therefore Formal Essays, reports, academic papers I was tired. Therefore, I went home.
as a result Formal Essays, reports, academic papers I was tired. As a result, I went home.

Punctuation Rules

Connector Punctuation Pattern Example
so Sentence 1, so sentence 2. It rained, so we stayed inside.
therefore Sentence 1. Therefore, sentence 2. It rained. Therefore, we stayed inside.
as a result Sentence 1. As a result, sentence 2. It rained. As a result, we stayed inside.

Key Differences

  • So: Connects two clauses with a comma; cannot start a sentence
  • Therefore / As a result: Start a new sentence; require period before and comma after
  • Meaning: All three express the same cause-effect relationship, just with different formality

Usage Tips

  • Use so in everyday conversation and informal writing
  • Use therefore in academic or professional contexts when you want to sound formal
  • Use as a result when emphasizing the consequence of something
  • Don't mix: ❌ It rained. So, we stayed inside. → ✓ It rained, so we stayed inside.
