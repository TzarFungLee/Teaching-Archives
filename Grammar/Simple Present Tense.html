Simple Present Tense

💡 Key Concepts

  • When to use: Habits, routines, general truths, permanent situations, scheduled events
  • Structure: Subject + verb (base form / +s for 3rd person singular) + object/complement
  • Examples:
    • I study English every day.
    • She works at a hospital.
    • The sun rises in the east.
  • Time markers: always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day/week/month, on Mondays
