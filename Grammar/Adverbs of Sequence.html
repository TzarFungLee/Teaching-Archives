📦 Concept Compressed

Adverbs of sequence (also called sequencing words) help organize events, instructions, or ideas in chronological order.

Common Sequence Adverbs

Position Adverbs Example
Beginning First, Firstly, Initially First, preheat the oven.
Middle Next, Then, After that Next, mix the flour.
End Finally, Lastly Finally, bake for 30 minutes.

Key Rules

  • Punctuation: Use a comma after sequence adverbs at the start of a sentence.
  • Variety: Don't repeat the same word; use synonyms to sound more natural.
