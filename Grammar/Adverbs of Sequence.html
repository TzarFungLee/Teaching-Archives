💡 Key Concepts

  • Purpose: Show the order of events or steps
  • Common sequence words:
    • First / Firstly - the beginning
    • Next / Then / After that - middle steps
    • Finally / Lastly - the end
  • Usage: Use these words to organize instructions, stories, or explanations

📖 Example: How to Make a Sandwich

Step Instruction
First, take two slices of bread.
Next, spread butter on the bread.
Then, add lettuce, tomato, and cheese.
After that, place the ham on top.
Finally, put the second slice of bread on top.

📦 Concept Compressed

Adverbs of sequence (also called sequencing words or time order words) help organize events, instructions, or ideas in chronological order, making communication clearer and more logical.

Common Sequence Adverbs

Position Adverbs Example
Beginning First, Firstly, To begin with, Initially First, preheat the oven to 180°C.
Middle Next, Then, After that, Afterwards, Subsequently, Following this Next, mix the flour and sugar.
Then, add the eggs.
End Finally, Lastly, In the end, Eventually Finally, bake for 30 minutes.

When to Use

  • Instructions/Procedures: First, turn on the computer. Next, open the program. Finally, save your work.
  • Stories/Narratives: First, we arrived at the beach. Then, we set up our tent. Finally, we went swimming.
  • Explanations: To begin with, the economy was stable. Subsequently, inflation increased. Eventually, the government intervened.
  • Process descriptions: Initially, water is heated. Then, it evaporates. Finally, it condenses.

Example Story Structure

First, I woke up at 7 AM. Next, I took a shower and got dressed. Then, I ate breakfast. After that, I drove to work. Finally, I arrived at the office at 9 AM.

Key Rules

  • Punctuation: Use a comma after sequence adverbs at the start of a sentence (First, ... / Then, ...)
  • Position: Usually at the beginning of a sentence or clause
  • Variety: Don't repeat the same word; use synonyms (First → Next → After that → Finally)
  • Logical order: Ensure events follow a sensible chronological sequence
